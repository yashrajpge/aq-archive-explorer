Air Quality Archive Explorer

Browse historical air-quality records from the OpenAQ open-data archive, station by station, with each station's actual data coverage shown up front.

Historical data, honestly labeled. This tool reads the OpenAQ archive of past measurements. It does not show live or current conditions. Every station lists the exact months the archive holds for it, and badges mark how stale the most recent archived month is.
